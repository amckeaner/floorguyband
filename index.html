About

Floor Guy is four friends from Hailey, Idaho, making music that gets the whole room swaying.

Emily Freudenberger writes the songs: stories rooted in place, funny one minute and earnest the next. She plays acoustic guitar and piano. Andrew McKean and Sam Dispenza fill in around her, trading bass, guitar, pedal steel, mandolin, banjo and the occasional train whistle. Koko Furlong keeps the whole thing steady from behind the kit.

They don’t pick a genre and stick to it. They follow whatever sounds good and feels good to move to, and they’d rather sink the floor than raise the roof. Come ready to dance, maybe cry a little, and have a good time.
